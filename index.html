about

DOIDOIis a Seoul-based media artist exploring how invisible systems shape perception and reality.

Working across interaction, computational media, and physical interfaces, DOIDOI investigates how images, data, technologies, and rules become tangible through the body and experience. Rather than treating technology as a tool for representation, DOIDOI uses it to design the conditions through which an experience can feel real.

With a background in visual communication design and interaction development, the practice combines artistic research with technical experimentation, examining the shifting relationships between perception, technology, physical space, and human behavior.
